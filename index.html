Introduction

Hello! I am Mr. Dip Narayan Dahal, an English teacher at Shree Himalaya Secondary School, Nishankhe, Okhaldhunga. I teach English to students from Class 9 to Class 12. With more than a decade of teaching experience, I am passionate about teaching English and helping students develop confidence, accuracy and creativity in language use.

This website is designed as a learning resource for students. It will provide useful English grammar lessons, English learning materials, books and previous examination questions in one convenient place.
